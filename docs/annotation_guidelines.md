# Annotation guidelines

This document is an initial framework. Final label definitions and examples
require review by fluent Sinhala and Tamil annotators before benchmark use.

## Principles

- Prefer questions with a clear, verifiable answer and cite authoritative
  evidence with provenance.
- Preserve the intended meaning and register of each language; do not rely on
  machine translation as the sole source of examples.
- Avoid ambiguous wording, culturally biased assumptions, and personally
  identifying information.
- Record language, category, subcategory, difficulty, evaluation method,
  annotator identifiers, split, and benchmark version for each item.
- Keep test examples private and do not use them for prompt or model selection.

## Review and adjudication

Annotators should independently review correctness, clarity, language quality,
evidence sufficiency, and safety. Disagreements must be recorded and resolved
through a documented adjudication process. Preserve both the initial annotations
and the adjudicated result.

## Placeholder status

Any generated starter example must be visibly marked `TODO` and excluded from
research claims until native-language review and adjudication are complete.
